F23 · Endpoint mobility without infrastructure routing control
DefinitionSupports an endpoint changing attachment or access network while preserving its peer identity and ongoing logical communication, without requiring the application to establish a new peer relationship or reconstruct communication. The endpoint must not need administrative control of infrastructure routes, prefix origination or an AS; moving an administratively controlled network with the endpoint does not qualify. Normal participant attachment, path authentication and automatic protocol signaling are allowed, including signaling intrinsic to a mesh or overlay; merely running routing code does not disqualify a participant. Ordinary network access permission is not authority to originate infrastructure routes. Evaluate a supported endpoint role and state its limits; client-only support can qualify. Mere route repair with stationary endpoints, or retaining an identifier while restarting communication, is insufficient. Lossless handover, simultaneous mobility of both endpoints and indefinite disconnection are not required. Only mechanisms in the declared stack count.

G05 · Plausibility of global operation
DefinitionA credible architectural route to a connected network of at least 10 billion devices using contemporary equipment and realistic regional interconnection. Assess namespace, routing/discovery state placement, aggregation, control traffic, search work and update behavior in the selected complete stack. Distributed state and growing aggregate resources are allowed. Documentary mechanisms, resource models, proportional simulations and operational experience can support Yes; world-scale deployment or a full-scale simulation is not required. No requires an evidenced architectural or resource obstruction in the selected composition under the common setting; insufficient evidence remains Pending. Prior targets (1 s discovery, 1 h admission, 99.9% coverage, 5% of domestic 1 Gbit/s per direction, 64 GB per transit equipment and 1000 destinations/device/day) remain sizing references, not jointly certified pass/fail thresholds. Keep identities owned separate from destinations accessed. Use the same Internet-shaped regional infrastructure, population, access workload and contemporary equipment envelope for every composition. Protocol-specific routing, discovery, caching and placement policies may differ on that infrastructure. Do not substitute a more favorable physical topology, omit regions, silently add links or assume an independently chosen cache-hit rate. Count additional equipment and its placement explicitly under the same resource envelope. An evidenced inability to serve this common setting can justify No; impossibility in every imaginable topology is not required. Missing evidence remains Pending.
