I2P — selected stack

IPv4 / TCP / NTCP2 / I2P destinations and tunnels; locally supplied fresh RouterInfo bundle, numeric NTCP2 contacts and known destination keys; multiple active outbound tunnels with at least two remote hops, tunneled destination lookups; correct local clock; no online reseed or external naming service; isolated router/destination key contexts without shared family declarations; independent private IPv4 with i2np.allowLocal and local bootstrap/network configuration; concurrent-tunnel policy is architectural, ordinary Java per-pair selection is sticky; transit-only C08 role with optional identity-bearing traffic/debug history disabled G01 global deployment: conventional BGP-routed IPv4 Internet underlay, matching the IP/MQ global case; private deployments remain separate S03 capabilities.
