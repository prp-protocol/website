DTN / CGR–SABR — selected stack

IPv4 / TCP / TLS 1.3 / TCPCLv4 / BPv7 + BPSec, with CGR routing procedures from SABR CCSDS 734.3-B-1 (2019). Documentary composition; executable integration not yet qualified. Locally provisioned numeric peers, private IPv4, private-use ipn identifiers (RFC 9758), singleton destinations, supplied contact/range plans and local clocks. Participant-operated certificate trust with NODE-ID validation, no online OCSP, DNS or SNI dependency. Final-pair BCB-AES-GCM payload protection and BIB-HMAC-SHA2 verification with locally provisioned pairwise keys; no relay-held final keys. No application authentication wrapper, anonymity overlay, multicast extension or shared-reference directory. BPv7 mapping of SABR priority/critical metadata and custody-dependent behavior remains an explicit integration question, not silently credited.
