DTN / LTP — selected stack

BPv7 + BPSec / LTP RFC 5326 / Encapsulation Packet Protocol 133.1-B-3 / AOS virtual-channel packet service 732.0-B-4 (2021 documentary baseline, not latest edition); CGR/SABR 734.3-B-1 routing. LTP segment binding follows 734.1-B-1 Annex B. No UDP, IP, TCPCL or TLS. Bidirectional adjacent links, locally supplied contact/range plans, peer/engine mapping, clocks and keys; singleton BP endpoints. All-red block transfer selected; optional green-part service is not credited. Final-pair BCB-AES-GCM and BIB-HMAC-SHA2 protect payload and authenticate a configured remote security source; no relay-held final keys. No link encryption/SDLS, anonymity overlay, application authentication wrapper or independent-participant discovery directory. Executable bindings, BPv7/SABR metadata mapping and allocation rules remain unqualified. CCSDS mission conformance and unrestricted local engine/channel assignment are not asserted.
