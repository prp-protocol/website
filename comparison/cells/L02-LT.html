Rationale

DTN7-RS supplies a published patent-grant witness for the BPv7 layer common to this composition. At the pinned commit, package dtn7 0.22.0 identifies RFC 9171 and offers MIT OR Apache-2.0; COPYRIGHT attributes the project to Lars Baumgaertner. Selecting Apache-2.0 invokes section 3, an express royalty-free grant for contributor-controlled claims necessarily infringed by contributions alone or with the licensed Work. The README applies the dual-license terms to intentional contributions unless expressly stated otherwise. This meets the existing holder-bounded, implementation-bound L02 rule. These three cells share one BP-layer witness; they are not three independent grants or a complete-stack patent clearance.
