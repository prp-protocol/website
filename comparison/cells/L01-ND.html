L01-ND: Pending

Rationale

The official NDN packet specification v0.3 points to the public NDN-packet-spec repository. At db8f251512b586d328480959a9aae154ca719a1f, the README describes the specification and its build process; conf.py identifies the Named Data Networking Project copyright. The recursive repository tree has no LICENSE or COPYING file. These checks establish the canonical source but do not establish a general use/implementation permission. The NFD or ndn-cxx software license cannot automatically supply the missing specification grant. The official August 2015 policy additionally promises open-source or Creative Commons licensing for documentation and technical reports. Its own page carries CC BY 3.0, but it does not identify a specific license and grant scope for every separate packet, NDNLP and management specification in this column.

Assessment method

Documentary grant-scope inventory; no legal clearance or patent search

Source context

Official NDN IPR policy v2 (August 2015), policy index, consortium terms and GPLv3 section 11

Outstanding qualification

The official openness policy is now located; the remaining issue is mapping concrete grants to the selected specification files. A future-facing licensing policy and a website footer do not by themselves establish coverage of distinct documentation repositories. Preserve the earlier pinned repository findings. No prohibition or field-of-use restriction is inferred from this unresolved mapping; implementation-code licensing and patents remain separate.
