Rationale

The approved criterion now fixes 1-second existing-destination discovery, 1-hour ingress, 99.9% deadline coverage, domestic control/unsolicited traffic below 50 Mbit/s per direction and routing state below 64 decimal GB per transit equipment. Distributed state is allowed with no aggregate memory or monetary ceiling; no single equipment may require a complete participant table. The selected stack has not yet been qualified against these simultaneous limits. Include the same PRP costs plus UDP/IP transport and the declared global Internet control plane; do not bound PRP identities by IPv4 address count. Round171 evaluates an agreed Internet-calibrated regional deployment, not every imaginable construction. All quantitative bounds remain. Regional calibration alone establishes neither capacity nor failure; previous arbitrary star/tree failures do not automatically become No.
