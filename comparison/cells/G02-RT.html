G02-RT: Yes

Rationale

For an initiating client changing a local attachment while its destination remains reachable, a path request can be answered from a transport node's existing destination cache on the requesting interface, with rebroadcast blocked. This bounded repair needs no network-wide announcement of the initiating client. It establishes a new Link, not seamless continuation of the old one.

Primary specification / explicit profile inference

Outstanding qualification

Move the initiating client between authorized serial attachments while the same destination route remains cached. Capture the interface-scoped response and new-Link establishment. Unknown-destination recursive search, moving destinations and convergence under broader failures are separate cases; no runtime test.
