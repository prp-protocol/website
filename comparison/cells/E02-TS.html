E02-TS: Yes

Rationale

Lipp, Blanchet and Bhargavan (INRIA, EuroS&P 2019) publish a mechanised cryptographic analysis of WireGuard handshake and transport messages, with public CryptoVerif models. The selected Tailscale data plane uses this protocol; its pinned wireguard-go dependency identifies Noise_IKpsk2_25519_ChaChaPoly_BLAKE2s. This is independent analysis of a selected security-critical protocol mechanism, not merely of a cipher primitive. E02 accepts bounded mechanism coverage, as already applied to TLS, QUIC and BPSec compositions.

Assessment method

Published third-party protocol analysis mapped to a pinned selected dependency; no proof rerun

Source context

Lipp, Blanchet and Bhargavan, EuroS&P 2019; author-hosted models and Tailscale wireguard-go e61b6b78110b

Outstanding qualification

Protocol-model analysis, not an audit of the current Tailscale fork, managed coordination, DERP, identity-provider enrollment or Tailnet Lock. Model assumptions and compromise cases remain relevant; no post-quantum or secret-PSK guarantee is credited to the selected no-additional-PSK profile. The authors report a Curve25519-model correction in their longer version and provide corrected model artifacts; the conference PDF is retained as historical analysis, not treated as an unqualified current security proof. Proof scripts were not rerun. The authors are distinct from the selected implementation team; the paper acknowledges discussion with the WireGuard designer, not independent certification of the complete product.
