E05-ND: Yes

Rationale

The US Department of Energy reports on 26 January 2023 that customers had deployed Operant solutions across more than 3 GW of energy infrastructure, and distinguishes commercialization from the earlier development and demonstration work. Operant's technical whitepaper explicitly identifies NDN as the networking approach used in its OT solution. Its 7 August 2023 account further describes customer use as a primary network for power-generation facilities. Together these support practical customer adoption of an NDN-based commercial solution beyond protocol research demonstrations.

Assessment method

Cross-source adoption inference with explicit attribution and unknowns

Source context

US DOE deployment report and Operant technical/deployment publications

Outstanding qualification

Adoption-level Yes: public DOE and provider-attributed customer deployment, not a customer-by-customer independent audit. The customers are described as energy operators rather than protocol researchers, but their individual names and affiliations are not enumerated in these sources. The provider-to-protocol mapping combines the deployment account with its technical description; no packet capture or source-version identification was obtained. Exact NDN/NFD version, transports, crypto suites and individual site duration are unreported. GW describes energy infrastructure served, not network throughput, node count or global scalability. No claim that every installation uses the selected column's complete profile; no vendor security comparison or performance claim is imported. No change to E01, E02, E03 or G05.
