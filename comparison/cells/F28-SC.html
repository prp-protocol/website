F28-SC: Yes

Rationale

SCION endpoints obtain path segments and combine them into packet-carried forwarding paths. This provides explicit selection of AS-level intermediaries rather than only a destination or local interface.

Assessment method

Primary documentary capability assessment; no new runtime experiment

Source context

Primary protocol specification or pinned implementation; selected-composition assessment

Outstanding qualification

Selection is among paths exposed by the control infrastructure and authorized segment information. It does not control hidden intra-AS or IP-underlay hops, or remove trust-root dependencies.
