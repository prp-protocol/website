F27-RA: No

Rationale

The selected IRATI composition does not supply an agreement on receiver availability windows. Its flow-allocation request carries QoS and DTP/DTCP configuration; the reviewed default, round-robin and QTA allocators select QoS cubes by delivery/retransmission requirements, rotation or delay/loss. DTCP windows represent sequence-number credit, while its time period paces a sending rate. DTP inactivity policies prepare a data-run flag after idle time. These mechanisms do not agree when the receiver will be available. The selected Wi-Fi mobility controller manages scanning, association and reassociation, not an availability-window negotiation. F27 is No for this composition, based on those defined mechanisms rather than the word window.

Assessment method

Selected-composition source review; distinguish flow credit and pacing from availability agreement

Source context

Pinned IRATI flow/QoS schema, allocation policies and Wi-Fi controller

Outstanding qualification

This is a source-level inference bounded to IRATI commit 726b535c908f8a74e8c68dd493cfbb1bd8726546 and the declared column. QoS extraParameters and policy descriptors are extension points, not a specified availability agreement. A separately defined sleep policy or carrier wake-time negotiation could change the result; none is selected here. This is not an impossibility claim about RINA, nor a denial of underlying Wi-Fi power saving. F24 is now assessed separately as No for the currently selected composition (revision 7.29). No runtime test, energy measurement or cryptographic-session retention claim.
