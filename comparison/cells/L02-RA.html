Declared royalty-free patent commitment

An attributable holder declaration or published commitment explicitly offers royalty-free implementation of the selected profile for applicable claims under the declaring holder's control. A recorded direct holder declaration is admissible for every project; publication or registry entry is reported separately, not required for Yes. State attribution, scope, conditions and publication status. This assesses the declared policy, not execution or enforceability of a patent license, third-party patent clearance, or copyright permission. An express patent grant attached to the selected implementation is admissible within its stated Work or contributor-version scope; it is not a promise covering every independent reimplementation. The same holder-bounded rule applies to direct declarations and public grants.

Rationale

IRATI at 726b535c908f8a74e8c68dd493cfbb1bd8726546 publishes its IPC API and flow allocator with LGPL-2.1-or-later notices. No recipient choice of a later GPL version is used to attribute a new patent grant to earlier contributors. Red Hat separately publishes a promise not to enforce its patents against specified activities involving Covered FOSS. Its coverage is not limited to Red Hat products or contributions. Mapping these public licensed components to that definition provides a concrete, holder-bounded non-assertion commitment under L02. The Yes is limited to that named holder and covered software, not to a newly discovered declaration by this protocol project.

Assessment method

Documentary mapping to an express named-holder non-assertion policy; not a project-owner or universal patent grant

Source context

Red Hat published Patent Promise, 21 September 2017; pinned public implementation license notices

Outstanding qualification

Credit only Red Hat LLC and its subsidiaries under the published 2017 Patent Promise, not a commitment by the protocol authors or all contributors. Matching public source and an Approved License are required. The promise excludes standalone hardware and noncovered software; combinations have additional scope conditions. Patent proceedings by the beneficiary or its affiliates against Red Hat, its affiliates or their offerings can remove protection, including for past actions. No third-party clearance, patent validity, existence of an applicable patent, enforceability opinion or complete dependency audit is established. This is a published non-assertion policy, not an inferred GPL/MIT patent clause. It does not cover every proprietary independent reimplementation. L02 accepts a holder-bounded declared commitment; no project-wide licensing endorsement or complete executable-profile qualification follows.
