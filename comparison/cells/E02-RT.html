E02-RT: No

Rationale

The official project cryptography page, retrieved on 20 October 2026, explicitly states that Reticulum has not been externally security audited. The public audit discussion describes organizing a review and its proposed scope, rather than publishing a completed report. No qualifying independent formal analysis of this networking stack was located in the bounded review. The dated evidence inventory is therefore No on the basis of the project's declared audit status, rather than search silence alone. A security paper about a different blockchain protocol named Reticulum does not qualify.

Assessment method

Dated project-declared audit status with bounded independent-analysis search

Source context

Official Reticulum cryptography page and public audit-planning discussion

Outstanding qualification

A dated evidence-maturity assessment, not a finding of insecurity. The official page does not give a publication date or explicitly enumerate every possible formal analysis; an unlocated report could supersede this inventory. Reassess when a published independent audit or formal analysis covering security-critical mechanisms of the selected profile is supplied. Unpublished work and primitive-level reviews are not established by this finding.
