G05-RT: Pending

Rationale

Global operational capacity is not demonstrated under the approved G05 limits. Native scoped discovery, regional announcements and cyclic-mesh delivery work, including full Link exchanges in the closing fixtures. Failed star/tree scenarios do not establish a universal No: a rootless analytical mesh avoids their necessary central bottleneck, although its complete native capacity remains unqualified. Address-space size, local success and configurable admission thresholds do not establish ten-billion-device operation with the required timing, coverage, domestic noise and full transit memory simultaneously. The defensible classification remains Pending, not Yes or No. This evidence review is concluded without claiming the underlying binary question has been resolved. Round171 evaluates an agreed Internet-calibrated regional deployment, not every imaginable construction. All quantitative bounds remain. Regional calibration alone establishes neither capacity nor failure; previous arbitrary star/tree failures do not automatically become No. Under the revised 1,000-destination daily workload, sixteen mixed-cache probes now compose native path_request with native request_path admission. Warm cached announcements schedule responses without recursive fan-out; unknown routes fan out to eligible modes. In a synthetic simultaneous burst, only the first unknown destination emits, but every unknown request is marked engaged; retries one second later emit nothing without resolution or expiry. A path entry whose cached announce is absent neither schedules a response nor falls back to recursive search in the tested branch. These controlled branch observations identify requirements for usable-cache accounting and burst recovery; they are not an end-to-end native deployment failure or universal No. Two subsequent native SerialInterface runs replace mocked branch execution with actual OS PTYs, worker threads, IFAC and peer replies. Paced requests discover and deliver to all 16 destinations; a 16-destination burst reaches only one, including after early retries and the tested delayed recovery schedule. No relay queue drops or IFAC/protocol violations were recorded. Raw local elapsed times are recorded per destination; this small SINGLE-delivery fixture does not qualify the selected global Link deployment, the corrected timing definition or 99.9% coverage. The burst result applies to this fixture and retry schedule, not all native configurations. Round 183 retains read-only snapshots of ingress burst flags, cooldown counters, request timestamps, discovery expiry and interface counters while extending caller retries to four-second intervals. All 16 destinations recover with exact deliveries after 55–115 seconds from first request; the original schedule reaches zero in this execution. Snapshots show ingress containment and discovery entries without downstream transmission, but do not isolate every suppression branch. These observations diagnose the small fixture; they do not establish a qualified global construction. Round 184 corrects the historical diagnostic field named engaged: it counted all discovery entries, not their engaged flag. Native logging and source inspection distinguish the 45-second in-flight request gate from the 15-second discovery lifetime; early batching can create an engaged=False entry without forwarding. See the trace and bounded interpretation in round184.html. Round 185 translates the regional daily-destination workload into conditional late-discovery, retry and pending-state budgets. The 99.9% deadline applies within the eligible cold-lookup cohort; warm accesses cannot dilute its failure rate. No fixture failure fraction is extrapolated to global traffic. Round 186 measures source-shaped native pending-table allocations separately from duplicate-request tags in six fresh CPython subprocesses. The native tag sets rotate by count rather than by the 45-second in-flight lifetime, so their synthetic all-retained footprint is not extrapolated as steady-state per-target memory. This is a representation measurement, not live-daemon capacity. Round 187 joins the measured pending-table coefficient with the existing equal-origin-group working-set model for the ten largest operator-region groups in each ten-billion-device scenario. It distinguishes coverage-compatible late-cohort accounting from an all-requests-held memory stress case. These remain conditional origin-gateway representations, not physical placement or full transit memory.

Assessment method

Three native Reticulum processes per run, SerialInterface/pyserial over rate-enforced OS PTYs, per-destination timing and exact payload verification

Source context

Completed evidence assessment under approved G05 rules

Outstanding qualification

Measure the fraction of eligible cold lookups delayed past one second, including admission and retry waits, separately per regional/load cohort; qualify retry traffic and all routing-essential state on explicitly placed contemporary equipment. Conditional round185 budgets are not measured failure probabilities or a qualified construction.
