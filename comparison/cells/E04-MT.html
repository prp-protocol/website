E04-MT: Pending

Rationale

Hernandez Ortiz, Quiroz Puentes and Rugeles (Universidad Militar Nueva Granada), arXiv:2605.17063v1 (2026), report controlled Meshtastic modem-preset measurements with Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3.2 and RAK4631 devices. The method uses fixed/variable attenuation, 50 packets per step and packet-error, RSSI and SNR observations. Hardware and radio settings are described, providing concrete external experimental evidence. An additional 2026 replay/tamper paper has an explicit firmware version (2.6.11), but its deposited abstract selects shared-key channel traffic and a modified counter/HMAC variant. Thus it does not close the selected-profile measurement gap.

Assessment method

External evidence scope triage, not full-paper audit or local experiment

Source context

Publisher-deposited Crossref abstract and official Meshtastic advisory; full paper unavailable (HTTP 403)

Outstanding qualification

The inspected paper does not identify an exact Meshtastic firmware release or commit for the experiment. It isolates the physical link rather than demonstrating the selected 2.8.1 STRICT-signature/PKI composition or multihop behavior. Hardware revision V3.2 must not be mistaken for firmware version. Without that mapping, E04 remains Pending; do not transfer attenuation results to this profile or turn them into global-scale, security or field-range guarantees. Academic affiliations are disclosed; no separate conflicts investigation or local radio experiment was performed. The additional paper was not read in full: PDF and publisher page returned HTTP 403. Abstract-level packet-size/overhead claims are not sufficient evidence of reproducibility or applicability to the selected strict-signature PKI build.
