Declared royalty-free patent commitment

An attributable holder declaration or published commitment explicitly offers royalty-free implementation of the selected profile for applicable claims under the declaring holder's control. A recorded direct holder declaration is admissible for every project; publication or registry entry is reported separately, not required for Yes. State attribution, scope, conditions and publication status. This assesses the declared policy, not execution or enforceability of a patent license, third-party patent clearance, or copyright permission. An express patent grant attached to the selected implementation is admissible within its stated Work or contributor-version scope; it is not a promise covering every independent reimplementation. The same holder-bounded rule applies to direct declarations and public grants.

Rationale

The public Nostrmo and nostr_sdk repositories maintained by haorendashu publish GPLv3 license texts. The pinned application selects the inspected SDK commit through its packages/nostr_sdk gitlink and local package dependency. The SDK Event class explicitly implements NIP-01 event serialization, SHA-256 identifiers and Schnorr signing/verification calls. GPLv3 section 11 supplies an express royalty-free patent grant under each contributor's essential claims for its contributor version. This additional client/SDK witness satisfies the existing holder-bounded L02 rule without inferring a patent grant from nostr-rs-relay's MIT license.
