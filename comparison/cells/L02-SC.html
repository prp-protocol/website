Declared royalty-free patent commitment

An attributable holder declaration or published commitment explicitly offers royalty-free implementation of the selected profile for applicable claims under the declaring holder's control. A recorded direct holder declaration is admissible for every project; publication or registry entry is reported separately, not required for Yes. State attribution, scope, conditions and publication status. This assesses the declared policy, not execution or enforceability of a patent license, third-party patent clearance, or copyright permission. An express patent grant attached to the selected implementation is admissible within its stated Work or contributor-version scope; it is not a promise covering every independent reimplementation. The same holder-bounded rule applies to direct declarations and public grants.

Rationale

The pinned SCION implementation publishes Apache-2.0. Section 3 expressly grants royalty-free patent rights from contributors for claims licensable by them necessarily infringed by their contributions alone or with the Work. This bounded public commitment meets the same holder-scoped L02 rule used for PRP.

Assessment method

Express patent-clause and applicability review under the common holder-bounded L02 rule

Source context

Version-pinned project licenses and attributable source notices; documentary review 2026-10-17

Outstanding qualification

Patent litigation alleging infringement by the Work or incorporated contributions terminates the section 3 grant. Published implementation-bound grant, not a blanket royalty-free promise for arbitrary independent reimplementations. Credit only claims controlled by the declaring contributors within the grant; no third-party, dependency or underlying-layer clearance is inferred. License compliance remains required. No finding that a patent exists, is valid or is infringed, and no legal-enforceability opinion.
