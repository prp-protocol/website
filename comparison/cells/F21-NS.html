F21-NS: Yes

Rationale

For the explicitly selected event-author-to-reader role, NIP-01 binds the author public key to the signed event content; nostr-tools verifyEvent checks the canonical event hash and Schnorr signature. The verified event supplies its author identity to the receiving application. This authenticates the communication item's author, not the current relay, WebSocket publisher or reader, and does not establish freshness or live possession.
