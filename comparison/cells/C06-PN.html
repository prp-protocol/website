C06-PN: Pending

Rationale

The observer inventory establishes that wire-v4 route_alias is local to an adjacent session/generation; AOP encryption hides its RRL from a passive carrier observer. A participating adjacency terminator can decode that RRL. The retained wire section 7.1 requires transit to preserve the E2E annex byte-for-byte, and section 12 exposes the protected unit generation/sequence nonce while encrypting its plaintext. This does not by itself settle whether addresses and visible flow identifiers expose the final-peer pair across the selected observation points. The author-approved successor direction removes ONION, but its proposed forwarding/privacy model must not be silently substituted for the selected wire-v4 composition.

Assessment method

Selected-composition primary documentary review; no runtime experiment

Source context

Pinned current PRP specification and separately identified successor proposal

Outstanding qualification

Complete a per-observer established-packet inventory: passive carrier observer versus participating relay with its own adjacency/direct keys; which E2E envelope or identifiers each sees; local alias translation; and which observations associate those values with both final participants. Distinguish a common ciphertext/packet fingerprint from an address/flow identifier under C06 and timing/volume correlation under C07. Preserve the selected PI underlay's segment-address visibility. Do not infer a Yes solely from scoped aliases or encryption, or a No solely from unchanged opaque annex bytes. Bind any successor-based verdict to its actual selected forwarding contract. Bootstrap disclosure and new-session implementation acceptance are separate questions.
