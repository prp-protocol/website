G02-GN: Yes

Rationale

The existing-alternate witness needs no global routing update: a tunnel removes its failed connection and schedules payload on another ready connection. The affected path may carry failure/cleanup messages, and later discovery can use the DHT; neither is a mandatory network-wide dissemination prerequisite for switching to an already established alternate. This is endpoint/path-scoped recovery, not a claim that every intermediate relay locally splices a new path.

Assessment method

Pinned release-source analysis and primary protocol review; no runtime execution

Source context

src/cadet/gnunet-service-cadet_tunnels.c: GCT_connection_lost, get_ready_connection, trigger_transmissions; src/cadet/gnunet-service-cadet_core.c: handle_connection_broken

Outstanding qualification

Capture the whole bounded topology during single-path failure with an already-ready alternate. Verify recovery without a global update, recording path-local failure traffic separately from optional later discovery.
