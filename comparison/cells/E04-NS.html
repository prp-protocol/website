E04-NS: Pending

Rationale

A concrete published benchmark candidate is now retained: nostr-bench reports latency and event/query rates for several relays on Linux/Docker, with workload parameters and a named client version. Historical v0.1.1 source is recoverable, but relay versions are selected dynamically and the transport is plain WebSocket. Its query loops and missing-response accounting do not establish validated delivery for every reported sample.

Assessment method

Benchmark scope and reproducibility audit; no performance experiment

Source context

Retained report and historical benchmark source reveal concrete measurement qualifications

Outstanding qualification

Still Pending. Reconstruct exact relay images/source revisions, toolchain and environment; audit sample denominators, expected responses and event IDs; distinguish publication from retrieval and test the selected TLS/composition before applying these measurements to this column. The report is from the benchmark author, who also presents Wisp favorably; author independence must be established for the particular implementation being evaluated. No rank or quantitative performance conclusion is imported. The E01 bounded exchange finding does not satisfy E04 reproducibility and independence requirements.
