F25-PN: Pending

Rationale

The pinned pre-HS2 admission contract specifies 60-byte offer and 120-byte challenge payloads, each with one public-control kind byte. Mandatory admission carries M1 inside proof fragments of 60+N payload bytes plus that kind byte; this replaces ordinary public M1 transmission rather than adding a duplicate M1. For one successful adjacent admission with 1000-byte M1 segments, the incremental endpoint wire cost over round349 is 318–647 native Ethernet byte-times or 374–703 IPv4/UDP byte-times. Across identity suites, framed HS2 plus this admission totals 8034–39171 native, or 8482–40403 over IP. These remain partial sums. The contract separately allows method 0 to proceed directly to HS2 without challenge/proof, so PoW is not a universal mandatory cost. Wire-v4 section 15 adds a separately identifiable inner item subtotal: OPEN 17 + ACCEPT 23 + two 256-byte payloads + four 14-byte item headers = 608 bytes; four 6-byte record headers and four 2-byte list counts make 640 bytes before encryption/onion/adjacent/link wrapping. This is a two-direction candidate with 512 useful bytes, not a network total and not automatically additive to the earlier interface subtotal.

Assessment method

Conditional exact admission arithmetic and cold-bootstrap scope review; no protocol execution

Source context

Committed pre-HS2 PoW admission contract, PRP bootstrap/directional-carrier specifications and prior frame accounting

Outstanding qualification

Pending complete witness. Freeze actual local admission policy; one successful HASHCASH attempt is a conditional accounting case, not evidence of solver completion within challenge validity. No retries, expired challenges, extra E2E admission, full bridge activation or runtime validation are credited. Bootstrap specification sections 3.1 and 7.1 require fresh physical carrier evidence and exact current generation/TX readiness: retained identity/configuration does not license reuse of expired candidate handles. Directional-carrier semantics defines no carrier-specific framing, so fresh candidate reconstruction must be bound to a concrete carrier configuration before assigning a zero-byte discovery cost. Route/relation controls, useful data and enabled cover scheduling still need a bounded composition. Cover traffic remains optional architecturally but enabled in these columns; neither its omission nor a fabricated rate closes F25. This result narrows two byte-accounting gaps without changing profile, criterion or verdict. Further isolated arithmetic is deferred until a coherent candidate fixes fresh carrier discovery, route/control exchange count and bounded padding/cover policy. See round351-closure.json for exact missing inputs; no criterion relaxation or silent zero-cost term.
