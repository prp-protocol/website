Rationale

The selected ION source at 154a4cc publishes a Caltech BSD-style license permitting use and redistribution. Its root license has no express patent grant or non-assertion commitment. This copyright evidence alone cannot resolve L02. The finding is limited to this examined license; it does not establish that no separate holder declaration exists.

Assessment method

Documentary commitment review; no patent clearance or runtime experiment

Source context

Primary holder disclosures and pinned implementation license, reviewed 2026-10-17

Outstanding qualification

Locate an attributable patent-specific declaration or applicable express grant for the selected protocol or implementation. For CGR/LTP, prior CCSDS no-known-patent and RAND statements remain distinct from royalty-free commitments. Do not infer royalties, infringement or patent-free status from the absence of an express root-license clause.
