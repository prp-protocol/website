E05-TO: Pending

Rationale

Torservers reports operating Tor infrastructure since 2010 in its December 2013 announcement. The Tor Project independently identifies Torservers and Noisebridge as operators distinct from its software-development effort in October 2011. These records establish multi-year real relay operation, rather than merely software availability. They concern exit/bridge infrastructure, not the selected authenticated onion-service v3 endpoint.

Assessment method

Documentary evidence and profile matching; no runtime execution or operator contact

Source context

Primary operator statements and author-hosted research report

Outstanding qualification

Find a dated independent operator account covering at least six months of onion-service v3 with the selected client-authorization behavior. Relay deployment does not prove that endpoint configuration, and these historical records predate v3. Pending is specific to that evidence gap, not a statement that Tor lacks long-running independent users. Optional Conflux design credit is not a claimed deployed feature.
