C08-I2: Yes

Rationale

A transit-only tunnel participant removes HopConfig and forwarding entries when the tunnel expires. It need not archive final client destinations after completion. Select no identity-bearing traffic/debug history; router peer profiles and live reachability are infrastructure state, not a required final-client communication archive.

Assessment method

Documentary analysis

Source context

Primary specification / explicit profile inference

Outstanding qualification

Inspect expiry, optional logs/message history, netDb roles, peer statistics, dumps and backups in the transit-only configuration. No deployed logging or forensic-erasure audit; do not extend this finding to application endpoints storing client records.
