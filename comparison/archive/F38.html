F38 — archived previous definition

Historical assessment from revision 7.33, retired in 7.34. These 28 cells are excluded from the active grid and counts. F38 is not reused or redirected to a new definition.

Let each operator enforce local access and resource priorities

Each transport scope can administer its resources and establish and enforce local access and priority conditions, respecting agreements with participants, without requiring one uniform policy throughout the network. Identify the scope, policy owner, controlled resources and enforcement point. Scopes may be adjacent or nested; recursive layers and hidden internal topology are not required. Configurable local preferences alone do not suffice without control of the relevant resources. F36 separately assesses participant-requested service quality, and F37 response to congestion. Local autonomy does not imply unlimited resources, arbitrary violation of agreements or absence of higher-level constraints.

Assessment method

Primary evidence for the selected composition; identify mechanism, scope and implementation limits

Research action

Identify independently administered transport scopes, local access/priority policy and resource enforcement. Explain how different local conditions can coexist along a communication. Do not require nesting, topology abstraction or RINA-style layers. Distinguish local policy authority from the ability merely to request QoS.
