More Than One Path

Lia talks to Davi while a tram crosses the city. A proposed client-led mobility architecture illustrates preparing a new authorized access, temporarily sending duplicate data over two paths and discarding repeated copies at the receiver.

Complete narration

0:00 · The conversation travels with Lia

Lia is crossing the city while talking to Davi. Along the way, one tower's signal weakens and another grows stronger. In this proposed mobility architecture, the device itself guides the change of access.

0:15 · Prepare the next access

Before leaving the first tower, the device prepares access through the second. A stronger signal is a reason to try. Authorization and an available path allow it to start using that access.

0:30 · The same data over two paths

During the transition, PRP can carry the same data over two authorized paths. The redundancy is deliberate: one copy can arrive even when the other encounters a failure along the way.

0:44 · Receive once

At the destination, the copies are recognized as the same data. The duplicate is discarded before becoming a second delivery to the application. The paths change, but the communication remains bound to the same participants.

1:00 · Complete the handover

When the new path is ready to support the communication, the device leaves the previous one. In this architecture without a mobile core, the handover decision belongs to the client. The relationship with Davi remains.

1:15 · Use redundancy when it helps

Using two paths consumes more resources. Redundancy can therefore be limited to the transition. Its protection depends on alternatives that remain available when one path fails.

1:29 · The relationship remains

Prepare the next path before leaving the previous one. Mobility with continuity built around the relationship. PRP.
