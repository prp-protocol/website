Complete narration

One off-screen narrator speaks throughout. The text transcribes the narration, not an inventory of music or sound effects. Scene times are navigation markers.

0:00 · An account between two people

Why should talking to someone depend on both of you having an account with the same company?

0:06 · Participants and relationships

Meet PRP: the Participant Relationship Protocol. A shared set of rules for communication, built around participants and their relationships. Not accounts granted by a central platform.

0:21 · Choosing a connection

Your identity is separate from the network carrying your messages. You can change connectivity providers without rebuilding your relationships. The provider carries the connection; it does not define who you are.

0:36 · A compatible home device

The same idea reaches your home. A compatible device can communicate with you without requiring a manufacturer's account or cloud service in between.

0:48 · Closing

Not another platform to join. A foundation for more autonomous, private communication. PRP. Your relationships, beyond any single platform.
