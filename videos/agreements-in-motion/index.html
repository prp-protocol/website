Complete narration

0:00 · Before the journey

A ride begins before anyone gets into a car. Two people. Different needs. Conditions of their own.

0:12 · Conditions already chosen

The passenger has set her budget and pickup preferences. The driver has chosen where to work and the terms he accepts. Each has already authorized an application to act within those limits.

0:26 · Discovery and quotations

A shared alias helps the application find nearby drivers. It establishes relationships with the participants. One request then reaches them through multicast. Across thousands of possible drivers, those available for this journey return their quotes. The application compares the offers within the passenger’s saved preferences.

0:54 · Applications reach agreement

There is no central dispatcher. Their applications work out the ride directly, within the conditions each person has already chosen. When compatible terms are found, the agreement forms automatically. The scheduled pickup appears for the driver. No extra acceptance step.

1:14 · Boarding

The car arrives. A greeting, an open door, and the journey begins. The agreement serves the people, not the other way around.

1:31 · Communication and application

PRP provides the foundation for communication between participants. The application defines how offers and agreements work. People choose their tools and conditions; compatible applications coordinate the journey.

1:49 · Arrival and closing

People set the conditions. Applications find agreement. Life moves forward. Built on PRP.

Context and credits

Approved final edition, M5. This is an illustrative application scenario, not a released ride service. PRP supplies communication; the application defines offers, matching and agreements. Compatibility and prior authorization on both sides are required.
