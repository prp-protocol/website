0:00 · 01

At the checkout, Lia's phone receives a payment request. She has not given the shop her phone number or presented a card. How did the request find her?

0:11 · 02

Lia keeps her enrolled facial reference on her phone. Physical proximity lets a nearby checkout send her a request, without prior approval of the shop.

0:23 · 03

The checkout application derives similarity clues from a facial reading. These clues can resemble those associated with Lia's reference, even when the two images are not identical.

0:36 · 04

PRP's discovery summaries help choose promising directions toward possible matches. The search follows clues through the network, then checks the candidates.

0:49 · 05

Once the match is confirmed, the shop automatically sends the request. Her phone can verify the merchant's identity through a notary she trusts, helping her check who is asking for payment.

1:03 · 06

Her phone shows the shop, the purchase and the amount. Lia reviews the request and approves the payment. Her phone keeps a record of who asked, when, and what she decided.

1:17 · 07

The request finds you. The decision stays with you. PRP.
