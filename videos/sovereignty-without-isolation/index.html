Sovereignty Without Isolation

Complete narration

One off-screen narrator speaks throughout. The text transcribes the narration, not an inventory of music or sound effects. Scene times are navigation markers.

0:00 · Research across borders

A research team is working to make water safer. Their partners are in another country. Their work depends on being able to talk, compare results, and keep learning together.

0:12 · A blocks B

Then, a decision in A blocks the addresses of networks in B. The infrastructure is still working. But the direct path between the teams is blocked.

0:23 · A relationship carried by another

A participant in C offers another path. In PRP, one relationship can be carried by another. For A, the direct relationship is with C. B's identity does not appear as the source at that transport layer.

0:39 · C joins the blockade

A persuades C to block B as well. That closes the direct path between B and C. But joining the blockade does not reveal the origin of the relationships being carried.

0:53 · Another layer

Now, D carries the relationship with B through a relationship with C. C knows D. A knows C. The inner relationship remains protected. Blocking B by address is not enough to identify this communication.

1:08 · More participants, more paths

Another participant can offer another layer. Stopping the communication means closing the indirect paths too. That requires broader cooperation to enforce the blockade, or cutting off intermediaries and affecting other people.

1:24 · The long chain is not the only option

This continuity depends on available paths and willing participants. Closing every way out can still stop communication. But blocking a country's addresses is no longer enough.

1:37 · Sovereignty without isolation

The sovereignty being protected is the freedom to keep choosing your partners. Sovereignty without isolation. PRP.

Context and credits

Conceptual film, M4, using fictional jurisdictions A–E. The viewer sees the full diagram; each participant's direct transport relationship exposes only its own context. Continuity depends on available paths and willing participants. This is not an experiment, a universal anonymity claim or a guarantee against every blockade. Identities known at the client's last hop are not automatically known to the ISP.
