Complete narration

One off-screen narrator speaks throughout. The text transcribes the narration, not an inventory of music or sound effects. Scene times are navigation markers.

0:00 · Identities instead of numbers

Imagine a device built for PRP. No SIM card. No phone number. You connect through identities and relationships. Personal life. Work. Different contexts, different identities. You choose who you communicate with, not a number assigned by an operator.

0:20 · A device out of the box

A home device can arrive with its own identity, created at the factory without consulting an authority or receiving a delegation. Take it out of the box. It needs no manual internet setup, SIM, or manufacturer's controller. Scan its printed QR code to establish your relationship— not to activate its connection.

0:44 · Several providers and technologies

The same connectivity provider can serve you on behalf of several operators, across different connection technologies. An operator becomes one option among others, not the exclusive owner of your access. Your relationships are not tied to a single subscription.

1:03 · Home, city and satellite

At home, Wi-Fi connects through your local equipment. Across the city, antennas provide metropolitan coverage. Satellite links offer another way to reach wider networks. Different equipment, different reach. More than one way to carry the same relationships.

1:22 · Neighbors form a mesh

Devices can also connect directly through Wi-Fi Direct. Subscribers can form mesh networks, carrying communication through participating neighbors. Each chooses whether to contribute and on what terms. The network grows through participation, not through a mandatory central operator.

1:43 · Continuity along the journey

As you move, connections and providers can change. PRP separates the relationship from the path carrying it. Where another authorized path is available, communication can continue that way. You do not need a new phone number, or to rebuild your relationships.

2:02 · A city sensor

The same autonomy extends to smart cities, from street equipment to flood sensors.

2:09 · An authorized group

Across a city, civil defense can reach thousands through an authorized group. PRP groupcast provides native multicast across operators: the message branches through the network, rather than requiring a separate send to every recipient. For field teams, push-to-talk carries a speaker's voice to the group, even across different operators.

2:35 · Arrival and closing

You arrive. The conversation continues. No SIM card. No telephone numbering plan defining how people reach you. Identities and relationships, carried by networks you can choose and help build. Mobile, without a mandatory central operator. Connected, on your terms. PRP.
