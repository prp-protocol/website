One Person. Many Contexts. Your Choice.

One woman appears in family, work and community settings. The film illustrates choosing different identities for different relationships and authorizing continuity when devices or network paths change.

Complete narration

One off-screen narrator speaks throughout. The text transcribes the narration, not an inventory of music or sound effects. Scene times are navigation markers.

0:00 · A person in several contexts

You are many things to many people. A familiar face at home. A colleague at work. A voice in a community. Each relationship has its own context.

0:12 · A shared dependency

But online, those contexts can end up tied to a single account. One profile. One provider. One place deciding whether you can still be reached.

0:24 · The account becomes unavailable

When that account becomes unavailable, more than a login can disappear. Connections you depend on can disappear with it.

0:35 · Choosing contexts

PRP starts with your autonomy. You can use different identities in different relationships. There is no requirement to place every part of your life under one universal identifier.

0:49 · Different recognition

Each relationship has its own rules for recognition and access. You choose which identities to use, which services to trust, and which contexts to keep separate.

1:04 · Authorized continuity

With continuity authorized, a new device or a different network need not mean starting over. The path can change while the relationship remains. Keeping contexts separate does not make every trace invisible.

1:20 · Your choices

Your identities. Your relationships. Your choices. PRP.

Visual description by scene

Editorial visual descriptions for reading, not an audio-description track.

0:00–0:12 · A person in several contexts

A woman in a coral jacket and violet top stands in a bright kitchen. A phone on the table shows a grey-haired family member. Other scenes place her with a colleague at work and with a woman holding a book in a community reading space.

0:12–0:24 · A shared dependency

The family, work and community scenes become three labeled panels. Lines from them converge on a box labeled “Provider account”, visually representing services that share one account.

0:24–0:35 · The account becomes unavailable

The account box changes from available to unavailable. The three panels remain visible, but their dependent connections are marked unavailable. The diagram shows how one lost account can affect several relationships.

0:35–0:49 · Choosing contexts

The woman appears next to a conceptual context selector with Family, Work and Community. Work is selected. The three scenes then stand separately without the shared provider-account box.

0:49–1:04 · Different recognition

Family, work and community vignettes show different relationship labels. The family name is Maya; the community pseudonym is Riverleaf. Work identifies a team member. These labels are shown within their own contexts rather than joined into one universal identifier.

1:04–1:20 · Authorized continuity

An existing phone and a new laptop show the same family contact. A visible approval on the existing device illustrates authorized continuity. The path line changes while the contact remains. Text notes that the other contexts are not linked and that separate contexts do not make all traces invisible.

1:20–1:30 · Your choices

The closing presents the official PRP logo, “One person. Many contexts. Your choice.” and prp-protocol.org on a cream background.
